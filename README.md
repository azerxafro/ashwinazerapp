# ASHWIN AZER // OFFICIAL 4D ARTIST PORTAL & EPK ⚡

> **Official artist portal for independent music artist Ashwin Azer (Lucid ASH).**  
> Visual Aesthetic: **Punk Zine / Grunge Risograph / Xerox Pop Art** with continuous 4D spatial audio-reactive geometry.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Strict Tritone Palette

- **Primary Flood:** Acid Yellow (`#EEFF00`)
- **Deep Toner:** Ink Black (`#090909`)
- **Misregistration Accent:** Risograph Purple (`#7B2CBF`) — offset chromatic shadows, edges, and glitch layers
- **Sticker Cutout Outline:** Raw Paper White (`#F7F7F2`)
- **Print Artifacts:** Global animated SVG grain, photocopy toner streaks, faint paper fold crease overlays, and halftone dot-matrix rasterization.
- **Typography:** Ultra-bold brutalist sans-serif (*Syne*, *Anton*, *Archivo Black*) paired with cassette J-card monospace (*Space Mono*, *JetBrains Mono*).

---

## 🔮 4D Spatial Architecture & Audio Engine

1. **4D Audio-Reactive Hero Portrait & Tesseract:**
   - Layered 3D cutout of Ashwin Azer (`hero-portrait.png`) rendered through custom GLSL Halftone + Risograph shaders.
   - Intersecting 4D Hypercube (Tesseract) wireframe projected into 3D space with real-time 4D rotation matrices across $XW, YW, ZW, XY$ planes.
   - 4D Simplex Noise (`snoise(vec4(x, y, z, time))`) warping halftone dot density and ink bleed over time.
   - 3 separated Z-depth plates (Acid Yellow base, Purple misregistration, Black halftone foreground) with damped spring physics on cursor movement, mobile gyroscope, and touch-drag.
2. **Audio-Driven 4th Dimension (Web Audio API):**
   - Stark brutalist `SOUND [OFF / ON ⚡]` toggle.
   - FFT spectrum analyzer driving bass hits (Z-depth displacement), mids (tesseract rotation acceleration), and highs (purple/yellow risograph glitch flashes).
   - Embedded procedural underground synth engine providing immediate bass, snare, hi-hats, and synth riffs for previewing tracks.
3. **Temporal Z-Axis Scroll & Photocopy Flash:**
   - Screen-wide photocopy exposure flashes during major section transitions and audio peaks.
4. **4D Morphing Track Visualizer:**
   - Interactive 3D sphere mesh deformed by 4D simplex noise fields and shaded with real-time halftone dots in Section 02 (The Vault).

## 🏛️ Site Sections

- **01 // HERO:** Full-viewport Acid Yellow canvas, 4D interactive Ashwin Azer cutout + rotating wireframe Tesseract, monumental brutalist wordmark, audio telemetry, and live ticker tape.
- **02 // THE VAULT (Discography):** Tilted zine flyers / cassette sleeves for *THE AZER (Vol. 2)*, *DESTINY*, *Legends & Lovers*, and *THE AZER (Vol. 1)*, paired with the 4D Morphing Visualizer and brutalist mini-player.
- **03 // VISUALS & ARCHIVE:** Asymmetric collage grid of stills, album artwork, and video frames. Default B&W Xerox halftone transitions to vivid acid-yellow and risograph-purple tritone on hover via animated photocopy scan-line.
- **04 // THE DOSSIER:** Torn press clippings and typewriter notes pinned with black gaffer tape. Artist manifesto, WallMag Rap Awards 2026 Top 250 Creator credentials, and Monadelta Productions milestones.
- **05 // TRANSMISSIONS:** Oversized ink-stamp buttons for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, MusicBrainz, downloadable EPK Markdown package, and booking contact form with photocopy-flash feedback.
